## 🌍 About the Project

Cities like Mohali face two connected water problems. Drains clog with litter and silt, and the problem is often noticed only once waterlogging begins in the monsoon. At the same time, households send reusable greywater straight down the drain.

**DrainWatch** brings both issues onto one easy-to-use web platform. Residents can report and track drain problems on a live map, and learn how to reuse everyday water safely.

## ✨ Features

### 🚰 Live Dashboard
- **Community report form:** choose a locality, add a landmark, pick an issue type and submit
- **Interactive Mohali map:** colour-coded markers for needs attention, moderate and cleared
- **Recent reports:** filter by area, mark reports as clear, and jump to a report on the map
- **Live counters:** reports needing attention and reports marked clear update instantly
- **Saved in your browser:** reports remain after a page refresh, with a one-click data reset

### ♻️ Greywater Management
- **Source guide:** which household water is suitable for reuse, and which to avoid
- **Reuse ideas:** plant watering, laundry water reuse and outdoor cleaning, each with safety tips
- **Water-savings calculator:** estimates litres reused per week and per month
- **Three simple safety rules:** use it fresh, keep it off food crops, choose mild products

### 🎨 Design
- Custom logo, clean green theme and rounded card layout
- Fully responsive on desktop, tablet and mobile
- Accessible controls, readable text and clear status labels

## 📌 Project Notes

- DrainWatch is a **front-end prototype**. Reports are stored only in your own browser and are not sent to any municipal authority.
- The three starting reports are example data, and map positions are approximate for each locality.
- Greywater information is general awareness guidance, not professional advice.
