A GOOD FIRST STEP

Water ornamental plants

Use suitable greywater promptly and apply it to the soil around established ornamental plants, not directly onto leaves. Avoid pooling and check that your detergent is plant-friendly.

Keep the water below the leaves and let it soak into the soil. Do not use greywater on edible leaves or root vegetables.

KEEP IT SAFE

Three rules to remember

01
Use it fresh

Do not store untreated greywater for long periods. Use promptly and empty containers after use.

02
Keep it off food crops

Avoid applying it to edible leaves, root vegetables, or any part of food that people will eat.

03
Choose mild products

Avoid water with harsh cleaners, bleach, or detergents unsuitable for plants. Never drink greywater.

Start small. A bucket used carefully for an appropriate purpose can be a first step. For plumbing changes or larger systems, check local rules and consult a qualified professional.
